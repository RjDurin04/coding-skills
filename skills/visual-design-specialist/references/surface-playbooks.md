# Surface Playbooks

Read only the sections matching the current product surface. These are lenses,
not templates.

## Choose The Surface Mode

- **Operate** — repeated tasks, comparison, state, speed, precision, recovery.
- **Persuade** — comprehension, trust, proof, differentiation, decision.
- **Read** — sustained attention, editorial hierarchy, navigation, references.
- **Experience** — expression, exploration, sequencing, atmosphere.

Many products mix modes. Name the dominant mode per route or region; do not let
an expressive shell degrade an operational core.

## Dense B2B, SaaS, And Dashboards

Prioritize task and exception hierarchy over decorative summaries. Preserve
comparison structures such as tables when cards would reduce scanability. Make
freshness, completeness, scope, permissions, and uncertainty visually explicit.
Use density deliberately, with stable columns, aligned numerics, explainable
priority, and clear detail-on-demand. Charts supplement decisions; they do not
replace labels, definitions, or accessible data views.

## Consumer And Mobile Products

Optimize for one-handed context, interruptions, short sessions, safe areas,
input changes, network variability, and first-time versus returning use. Keep
the primary task legible without turning every screen into a giant CTA. Respect
platform conventions when familiarity reduces error; depart only for a durable
product benefit. Treat permissions, empty states, saved progress, and recovery
as part of the visual story.

## Commerce And Marketplaces

Make identity, price, variation, availability, delivery, returns, seller or
source trust, and primary purchase state unambiguous. Imagery should reveal the
product rather than merely decorate the page. Preserve comparison and long
attribute content. Do not fabricate scarcity, ratings, reviews, customers,
discounts, delivery promises, or social proof. On mobile, media, options,
pricing, and purchase controls must coexist without obscuring content or zoom.

## Developer Tools

Favor information density, keyboard clarity, stable geometry, monospace only
where code/data benefits, and visible system state. Keep command, result,
diagnostic, diff, environment, and destructive-action hierarchy distinct.
Brand expression can live in typography, color, illustration, and empty states
without reducing code readability or operational feedback.

## Content-Heavy Publications

Design the reading rhythm before the home-page mosaic. Distinguish navigation,
headline levels, deck, byline, body, annotation, caption, quote, data, related
content, and live-update status. Test very long titles, mixed media, footnotes,
breaking-news treatments, and low-image articles. Typography and whitespace
should support comprehension, not imitate a magazine at the expense of digital
navigation and accessibility.

## Marketing And Acquisition

Lead with the visitor's decision and credible proof. A hero is useful only when
it clarifies value, audience, and next action. Product evidence, constraints,
pricing, risk, and objections should shape the sequence. Avoid generic gradients,
floating cards, fake dashboards, logo walls, and oversized claims unless the
brief and verified content justify them. Conversion never authorizes deception
or inaccessible pressure.

## Creative Portfolios And Expressive Surfaces

Define the creative thesis and the work's viewing needs. Use asymmetry,
typographic drama, spatial sequencing, or rich media when it strengthens the
author's identity and case-study narrative. Preserve discoverable navigation,
content access without motion, responsive reading, image alternatives, and
reasonable loading/failure behavior. Expression is successful when it makes the
work more memorable without making it harder to reach or understand.

## Data Visualization

Begin with the comparison, trend, distribution, relationship, or anomaly the
user needs. Choose an encoding that supports that task and data shape. Include
units, denominators, time range, source/freshness, missing values, uncertainty,
and accessible alternatives. Use direct labels and restrained series counts
where possible. Decorative complexity is not analytical depth.
